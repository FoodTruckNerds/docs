
## Facebook

Integrate with Facebook to perform following functions for a food truck's page:

- Update profile information:
	- **Missing information** (such as Social links) - If they connect our app to other social networks, we can fill in missing links and update their links when they change.
	- **Business address** (Facebook permits updating as frequently as necessary)
	- **Price-range** (based on menu items)
	- **Services** (Takeout, Outdoor seating, Online booking)
	- **Check-ins** (Facebook's native check-ins feature)
	- **Photos** (Automatic reposting of photos used in the app)
	- **Categorization** - If their page isn't categorized as a Food Truck, we update their categories. (Limited to 3 categories)
- Display suggestions on Facebook:
	- **Groups** - Show groups that may be relevant to them, based on city)
	- **Events** - Show Daily events or Upcoming events
	- **Likes** - Show them our Facebook page and let them like us
- Create posts:
	- **Initial Post** - Announcement of their food truck after registering on our app.
	- **Event check-ins** - (Not a post every time the food truck checks in, since they may be stationary). Every post links to the food-truck's profile on our app.
